PunchPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

PunchPulse ("the app") is a VR workout game for Meta Quest made by Kazi Mostofa Sakin. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data we collect

None. PunchPulse does not have accounts, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not send any personal information to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Your high scores, workout count, estimated calories and whether you own PunchPulse Pro are saved only on your headset (browser local storage). This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Motion data

The app reads your headset and controller positions in real time to detect punches. This data is processed on the device while you play and is not stored or transmitted.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Meta through the Meta Horizon Store. We never see or store your payment details. Meta's handling of your data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
