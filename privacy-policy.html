PunchPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

PunchPulse ("the app", "we", "us") is a virtual reality workout game for Meta Quest, developed by Kazi Mostofa Sakin, Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada. This policy explains what data the app collects, processes and stores, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can ask for it to be deleted.

1. Summary

  • PunchPulse has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • To sell and verify the optional "PunchPulse Pro" in-app purchase, the app uses Meta's in-app purchase service, which processes your purchase information.
  • Game progress (high scores, workout stats and whether Pro is unlocked) is stored only on your headset.
  • Headset and controller movement is processed in real time to detect punches and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • You can delete your data at any time, as described in section 7.

3. Legal basis and purposes

We process purchase and ownership data to provide the product you requested (performance of a contract) and to meet Meta's platform requirements. Game progress and motion data are processed only to make the game work. We never sell your data or use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Who data is shared with

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. processes in-app purchases, payments and ownership checks. Meta's handling of your data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy. We never see your payment card details.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the app's files. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not share data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Game progress stays on your headset until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records are kept by Meta according to Meta's policies, so that your purchase can be restored. We do not keep our own copy of purchase records on any server.
  • Motion data is not retained at all.

6. Security

All communication with Meta and our web host uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Because we do not run our own servers or databases, no player data is held by us outside your device.

7. How to delete your data

Data on your headset: uninstall PunchPulse, or clear its storage in your headset settings (Settings → Apps → PunchPulse → Clear storage). This immediately deletes your scores, stats and the local Pro flag.

Request deletion from us: email kazi.sakin@icloud.com with the subject "PunchPulse data deletion request". We will confirm within 30 days. Any data we have access to about you will be deleted, and we will explain what remains with Meta and how to manage it.

Purchase data held by Meta: you can view, download or delete your Meta account information through Meta's Accounts Center.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR, or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

9. Children

PunchPulse is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
